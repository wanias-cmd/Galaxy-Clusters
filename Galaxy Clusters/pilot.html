ODYSSEY

Pilot a crewed spacecraft from Earth to Mars. Thread the navigation gates, dodge asteroids, survive solar flares and brake into Mars orbit before your oxygen runs out.

How to play

  • Goal: fly through all 5 gates, then slow below 25 km/s inside the Mars ring and hold for 2 seconds.
  • Move: touch: left stick steers, THRUST accelerates, BRAKE slows. Keys: W thrust, S brake, arrows or A/D steer, Q/E roll.
  • Views: VIEW (or C) switches between the cockpit and a chase camera behind the ship.
  • Physics is real: in space you keep moving when you stop thrusting. Turn around and brake early.
  • Radar (top right) shows gates, asteroids and fuel cells around you. The amber marker is your next target.
  • Solar flares: a red alert gives you 6 seconds. Raise the SHIELD (or F). It drains power, so drop it afterwards.
  • Resources: fuel is spent by thrust and braking. Collect gold fuel cells. Oxygen only runs down. Hull damage is permanent.
  • Comms delay shows how long a radio message takes to reach Earth from where you are.
