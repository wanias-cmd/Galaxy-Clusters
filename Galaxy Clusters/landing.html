Rocket Landing

  • Land on the grey pad without crashing. Press 1 · 2 · 3 for the Moon, Mars or Earth.
  • W / ↑ / Space fires the engine. A D / ← → tilt the ship. Phone: use the buttons.
  • The engine pushes along the nose, so keep the ship upright before you burn.
  • The Stopping distance shows how high you must start braking. Burn too late and you crash.
