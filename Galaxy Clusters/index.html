How to play

  1. Pick a module. Click or tap any card on the hub. A preview panel slides in from the right.
  2. Try the slider. Drag the slider in the preview. The picture and the numbers update live. This is how you learn the science.
  3. Check the controls. The Controls box lists the keys (computer) and touch gestures (phone) for that module.
  4. Press Launch. The module opens full screen. On a phone, turn the device sideways.
  5. Earn your rank. Each module you explore fills the Explorer rank bar at the top. Explore all 5 to become Commander.
  6. Need help? Press How to play or Guided tour any time.
