Stargazing Night

  • Walk: W A S D (Shift to run), drag to look around. Phone: left thumb walks, right thumb looks.
  • Walk to the carpet. When the button appears, press E to lie down.
  • Click or tap any bright star to see its name, distance, temperature and more.
  • The sky is calculated from real star positions for your chosen city, today’s date and the time you pick. Try Time-lapse to watch Earth rotate.
