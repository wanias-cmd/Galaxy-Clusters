Planet Story Tour

  • Watch the Sun and all eight planets revolve in 3D. Every planet moves at its real relative speed: the closer to the Sun, the faster it goes.
  • Story mode flies you from world to world and tells each planet’s tale. Press Next or let it play on its own.
  • Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom, and click any planet to fly there and read its facts.
  • Use the time slider to speed up or slow down the clock. Narration is off by default.
