Black Hole Simulator

  • Drag the Mass slider. A heavier black hole has a bigger event horizon and bends light more.
  • Click space to drop a probe. Far away it orbits. Inside the last stable orbit (3×) it spirals in.
  • Press Fire light beams to see light curve. Beams that pass too close are trapped.
  • Background stars show the Einstein ring: gravity acts like a lens.
