Walk on Another Planet

  • Computer: W A S D to walk, Shift to run, Space to jump, drag the mouse to look.
  • Phone: left thumb to walk, right thumb to look, JUMP button.
  • Press 1, 2, 3 (or the buttons) for the Moon, Mars and Earth.
  • Jump on each world and compare your highest jump. Gravity is the only thing that changes.
